### Specific educational improvements

- **Water & Brush:** retains the reviewed ten-unit teaching. Each focus now has a relevant painted comparison, distinguishing contact, liquid delivery, concentration, surface moisture and dry appearance. Texture is not automatically a defect. Technical control diagrams are available secondarily.
- **Three-Value Planning:** observe relative lightness before object/hue names; prepare matching frames and a dry-tested value key; group connected masses; relocate one small accent with similar total dark area; compare intentions and transfer to colour. It does not prescribe a universally darkest focal point.
- **Hard, Soft & Lost Edges:** dry-paper baseline, comparable-load damp-paper test, then matching adjacent dry appearances for a genuinely disappearing interval. Preserve found edges, distinguish lifting/softening and uncertain surface conditions, stop instead of scrubbing a setting seam. No preview is claimed to prove a lost contour.
- **Observe Shapes & Perspective:** fixed viewpoint and envelope first, then compare truly related receding directions; isolate one slope change in a second drawing. Invented building art supplies an example, not measurements. Different direction families are not forced to one vanishing point.
- **A Quiet Landscape:** intention and open light; three-family plan; dry-test palette and loading; connected light sky and a safe pause; dry-sky boundary test before hill; connected near mass before texture; optional tested finishing mark **or stop**; reflection tied to intention. The first hill experiment explicitly waits for a dry sky and tests rewetting on scrap; a lifting/streaking sample leads to the dry-paper option.

Active reading/practice estimates now sum to 55, 40, 43, 34 and 80 minutes respectively. Waiting/drying and optional viewing are additional. Existing short-session alternatives remain available; a short visit is not represented as completion of the extended study. Project scheduling tests now expose that its longest 14-minute canonical step exceeds a ten-minute budget rather than silently pretending it fits.

### Representative before / after

Before: short actions such as making large value masses or placing a near bank were supported mainly by an idealised scene. After: the learner checks a stable reference, tests a dry value relationship, chooses a connected movement and names the result before adding texture.

> “If you choose dry paper or the dampening sample lifts or streaks, use a dry-paper hill in the painting and keep its value quiet.”

> “Keep the changed accent's area and value comparable. If you also want to change its size, save that for a separate third plan.”

> “Call it lost only if the neighbouring dry appearances actually merge.”

These are conditional actions, not assurances that the synthetic painting proves the physical process.

### Failure scenarios challenged in review

1. A beginner floods the small sky: the lesson distinguishes a freely wet pool from a setting passage; a rinsed/blotted damp brush may take up only the mobile excess while wholly wet. Otherwise stop. The pooling diagnostic checks load, contact, movement and area; the next fresh test changes one control.
2. New water creates a backrun in a partly drying hill/sky: the diagnostic checks lost sheen and relative wetness, discourages scrubbing, and reserves an earlier/more comparable-load test for fresh paper. The hill stage now takes the beginner through drying and a safe rewetting sample first.
3. The supposed lost edge still has a seam: compare adjacent dry values and the gap/outline, preserve the current test, and make a new pair rather than repeatedly rubbing it. No Louise blending chapter is used as proof of loss.
4. Structure improves only because the corner moved: compare the same envelope/viewpoint and isolate the receding pair. The generated building does not supply a measured vanishing point.

## Technical/editorial review and limitations

The source-level review checks actions, causal explanations, controlled comparisons, dry-result observations, conditional diagnosis, transitions and material caveats. The per-unit review in the validation JSON separates inspected implementation evidence from human artistic/material validation. A plausible generated mark is not evidence of the process that created it. Sizing, pigment behaviour, texture, brush capacity and climate vary; no universal ratio, angle or drying interval is prescribed.

Official YouTube API reference, parameter reference and service terms were fetched successfully over certificate-verified HTTPS during this execution. Their object-form absolute bounds, approximate seeking, minimum 200×200 viewport, origin/referrer, caption-preference and third-party rights requirements were reviewed. Manufacturer source requests to both supplied DANIEL SMITH pages returned HTTP 403 this time. The existing reviewed pilot report records its earlier successful inspection of those sources; that historical evidence is not claimed as newly retrieved here. No legal certification or material experiment is implied.
